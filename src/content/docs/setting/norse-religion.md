---
title: Norse Religion
---

The Norse people revere the **Aesir**, a mythical family called "the gods".

The **Jötnar** are another family, joined with the Aesir by  
marriage & friendship, divided by rivarly and war.

The Aesir mostly protect humans, the Jötnar mostly use their magic for chaos.

### The Main Gods

**Odin** - a god for the upper class. Wise, bloodthirsty, and ambitious.  
He decides the outcomes of battles, and recruits slain warriors for his army.

**Thor** - a god for the common people. Strong, brave, and true of heart.  
He is the Aesir's greatest warrior, and defends the world against the Jötnar.

**Frigg** - a goddess, the wife of Odin. Magical, prophetic, and a symbol of
motherhood.  
She knows the future and the wise listen.

### Other Important Beings

**Valkyries** - magical shieldmaidens who bring slain warriors to Odin.

**Loki** - one of the Jötnar. Loki is a liar, a coward, and a troublemaker.  
Though he is Oden's blood-brother, at Ragnarök he will betray the gods.

**Hel** - one of the Jötnar, she claims the dead left by Odin.  
At once alive and dead, Hel rules over the dead in a frosty realm below.

**Jörmungandr** - a serpent so large he encircles the earth.  
His every movement shakes the ocean and floods the land. He is the equal of
Thor.

**Fenrir** - a giant wolf betrayed by the gods.  
He is a son of Loki, and the reason Odin is raising an army.

**The Norns** - mysterious beings who carve the fates of all into the bark of
the tree Yggdrasil.
