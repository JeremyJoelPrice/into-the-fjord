title: Introduction
---

This game's default setting is The North, a fantasy mashup of Iceland &
Scandinavia:

- sweeping fjords, glacial fields, and volcanic wastelands.
- monsters are everywhere; people call them [Jötnar](/setting/norse-religion) &
  wonder why Thor hasn't appeared to crush them
- Christian missionaries and soldiers have arrived to replace the local faith

There are less cataclysmic opportunities for adventure as well:

###### Dwarven Halls

Below ground lie the abandoned halls of _Niðavellir_.  
Cathedral-like and filled with magical
[artifacts](/rules/magic/dwarven-artifacts), artistic masterpieces, and lurking
dangers.

###### Kings & Jarls

The ruling class often hires mercenaries to protect settlements  
from existential threats, and their own Shakespearean dramas.  
They pay well, but alliances are fragile and you often must choose a side.

###### Viking Warbands

Explore new lands for trade, raids & adventure by joining a band or starting
your own.  
Some are institutions, their members famous celebrities.  
Retired warbands may sell maps of new lands.
