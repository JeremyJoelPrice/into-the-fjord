---
title: Resting And Healing
---

[Guard](/rules/fighting/guard/) is restored after resting for a couple of
minutes.

[Fatigue](/rules/fatigue) clears after a full night's rest & a meal.

[Ability Scores](/rules/ability-scores) recover with a [Short Downtime](/rules/downtime/#short-downtime-up-to-2-weeks) of bed rest.

[Wounds](/rules/fighting/wounds/) have unique recovery conditions.
