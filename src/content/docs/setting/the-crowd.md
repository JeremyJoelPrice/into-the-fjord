---
title: The Crowd
---

With no official guards, the general populace must prevent crime,  
or every insult escalates into a blood feud.

**The Crowd** fulfills this role, representing  
background NPCs going about their business.

- always present, when the players are in public
- always watching, ready to intervene
- always gossiping, effectively a hive mind

The Crowd responds to trouble by first sending someone  
of higher social status to demand you descalate,  
and ultimately 1d6 battle-ready folks will appear.

The Crowd's preferred outcomes, in order:

1. You aknowledge your crime and pay a fine to the injured party
2. You are aprehended & brought before the Jarl for judgement
3. You are killed on the spot
