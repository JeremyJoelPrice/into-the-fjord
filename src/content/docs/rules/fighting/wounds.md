---
title: Wounds
---

Wounds are nasty, but you come back stronger.

You are Wounded when you fail your Might roll _(see
[Attacks & Damage](/rules/fighting/attacks-and-damage/))_

Lookup the Might damage you took below.

**1-2**  
_A lucky hit knocks you out._  
Roll [Might](/rules/ability-scores) each round to see if you wake up.  
On recovery: reroll your [Guard](/rules/fighting/guard) and replace your current
max, if higher.

**3-4**  
_A heavy blow ruptures your insides._  
You're down & need help in the next 5 minutes or there's a **Poor** chance
you'll recover.  
On recovery: reroll your **Guard** and replace your current max, if higher.  
If you were using **1d6**, use **1d8** from now on.

**5-6**  
_Your flesh is torn apart and blood soaks your clothes._  
You gain a [Fatigue](/rules/fatigue).  
To recover: [a full night's rest & a meal](/rules/resting-and-healing/).  
On recovery: reroll your **Guard** and replace your current max, if higher.  
Increase your Guard die according to this pattern: 1d6, 1d8, 1d10, 2d6 (max)

**7-8**  
_Fractured bones leave you semi-conscious._  
You are [Deprived](/rules/deprivation) until you spend a
[Short Downtime](/rules/downtime/#short-downtime-up-to-2-weeks) in bed rest.  
On recovery: roll **3d6**; if it's higher than your max
[Grace](/rules/ability-scores), it becomes your new max.

**9-10**  
_Your guts are hanging out, your wakefulness flickers._  
You are **Deprived** & crippled.  
To recover: spend a
[Long Downtime](/rules/downtime/#long-downtime-up-to-3-months) in bed rest.  
On recovery: roll **3d6**; if it's higher than your max
[Will](/rules/ability-scores), it becomes your new max.

**11-12**  
Roll 1d4\*:

|     |          |     |           |
| --- | -------- | --- | --------- |
| 1   | Left Arm | 2   | Right Arm |
| 3   | Left Leg | 4   | Right Leg |

_This limb is chopped off, crushed, or rendered useless._  
You need **immediate** help or you'll bleed to death within a few minutes.  
To recover: spend a **Long Downtime** in bed rest.  
On recovery: increase your **Will** by **1d6**.

\*If your character is four-legged or has wings, roll 1d6 when choosing a
limb.  
\*\*If your character is a snake, the end of their tail is chopped off.
