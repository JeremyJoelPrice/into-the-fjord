---
title: Skills
---

Sometimes success is just a matter of your character's knowledge and ability,  
and there are no **tactical** or **creative** decisions for a player at the
table to make.

You can use a [Fate Roll](/start-here/how-to-play/#fate-roll) to see how well
your character performs their skill.

| Some Example Skills |                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Animal Kinship      | deal with wild animals, connect to your spirit animal                  |
| Diplomacy           | persuade crowds of people, deal with highly influential people         |
| Dwarf-craft         | alter & create [Dwarven Artifacts](/rules/magic/dwarven-artifacts)     |
| Herbalism           | create potent mixtures                                                 |
| Hunting             | follow tracks, find food, move stealthily                              |
| Lockpicking         | pick locks & interact with advanced mechanisms                         |
| Mercantile          | find a seller of something obscure, do business during downtime        |
| Poetry & Music      | humiliate someone in a verbal battle, entertain a crowd, highly praise |
| Sailing             | control a ship during battles & dnagerous voyages                      |
| Wandering           | make your way safely through wilderness, finding food & shelter        |
