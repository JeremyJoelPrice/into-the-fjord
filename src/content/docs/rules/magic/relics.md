---
title: Relics
---

Most relics are mere loot to be sold or used for bragging rights.  
Bulky & awkward to carry, but very valuable, their trade is considered blasphemy
by Christians.

| Mundane Relics (all are [_bulky_](/character-creation/gear-tables)) | Value  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Silver Crucifix                                                     | 100 sp |
| Gilded statue of a saint                                            | 200 sp |
| Bejewelled Book of Psalms                                           | 300 sp |
| Bones of a saint (fragile)                                          | 500 sp |

| Magical Relics | Effect                                                                                    |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Angel's Sword  | Blade burns when wielded by the faithful,<br />handle burns when touched by the faithless |
| Holy Water     | Burns & banishes demonic spirits                                                          |
