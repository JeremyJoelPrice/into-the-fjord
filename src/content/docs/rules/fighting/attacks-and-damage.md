## Attacking a Target

1. **ROLL DAMAGE** using the weapon's damage die.

2. Target's **[ARMOUR](/rules/fighting/armour)** reduces the damage.

3. Target's **[GUARD](/rules/fighting/Guard)** absorbs the remaining damage.

## Taking Damage

If the target's Guard can't absorb the incoming damage,  
then they take real damage:

1. Target's **[MIGHT](/rules/ability-scores)** absorbs the remaining damage

2. Target **ROLLS MIGHT**:  
   **SUCCESS** means you're still standing  
   **FAILURE** means you suffer a [Wound](/rules/fighting/wounds)

### Example

_The shieldmaiden attacks, rolling **1d8** and getting a **6**._  
_Her enemy has **1** Armour, so he takes **5** damage._  
_His Guard is **3**, so it absorbs that much damage and reduces to **0**,_  
_leaving **2** damage which reduces his Might to **12**._  
_Because his Might was reduced, the enemy rolls Might_  
_He gets a **13**, so he's taken a **Wound**._  
_Because he took **2** Might damage, he looks up "2" on the
[Wounds table](/rules/fighting/wounds/)._

## Special Attacks

- Attacks made form a position of advantage are **empowered**, and roll a d12
  for damage
- **Impaired** attacks roll a d4
- When **ganging up** on one target, all attackers roll damage and **only the
  highest** roll counts
- **Area** attacks affect multiple targets; roll damage **separately** for each
- To **disarm** or **trip** an opponent, their **Guard** must be 0.  
  Then, use your action to force them to make a Grace roll.  
  On failure they fall, or drop their weaopn nearby.
