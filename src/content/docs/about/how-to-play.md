One player is the **Guide**, who runs the game.  
They are in charge of describing the game world and interpreting the rules.

Everyone else
[creates a character](/character-creation/how-to-create-a-character) to play.  
The characters work together as a team to achieve their goals.

**Normal play** is a conversation:

- The Guide describes the situation, and asks: _"What do you do?"_
- The players ponder, discuss & ask questions
- Players tell the Guide what they do
- The Guide might ask you to roll some dice
- The Guide describes the outcome of your actions,  
  how the situation has changed, and then it's back to: _"What do you do?"_

## Rolling Dice

We use dice to represent uncertainty, chaos, and luck in the fictional world.  
The terminology is simple: **1d6** means roll one six-sided die. Likewise
**2d6**, **1d8**, etc.

There are **three** kinds of roll:

1. [**Damage Rolls**](/rules/fighting/attacks-and-damage/) to attack someone
   with your weapon
    - 1d6/1d8/1d10; depends on your [weapon](/character-creation/gear-tables/#arms--armour)
2. [**Ability Rolls**](/rules/ability-scores#rolling-abilities) to avoid harm in
   dangerous situations
    - 1d20, roll under your Ability Score
3. [**Skill Rolls**](/rules/skills) to use your character's skills
    - 2d6 + bonus vs 7
