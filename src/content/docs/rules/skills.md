- Roll skill using 2d6, and add your skill's **bonus**
- Get a 7 or higher to succeed
- If both dice match, the result is **extreme**

## Advice for The Guide: Beyond Skills

Here is the full range of bonuses for Skill Rolls,  
with adjectives in case they help you choose one when needed.

| bonus | success | quality  |
| :---: | :-----: | :------: |
|  +3   |   92%   |  Superb  |
|  +2   |   83%   |  Great   |
|  +1   |   72%   |   Good   |
|  +0   |   58%   |   Fair   |
|  -1   |   42%   | Mediocre |
|  -2   |   28%   |   Poor   |
|  -3   |   17%   | Terrible |

You can use this roll in two much broader ways,  
effectively making it a universal roll for anything.

1. You can create on-the-fly stats for anything:

- A ship may have **Great** speed, but a hull in **Poor** condition
- A horse may be in **Good** health and have a **Superb** temperament

2. You can directly estimate success in any situation:

- You want to forage here? There's a **Good** chance you'll find something
- That plan sounds too risky, it has a **Mediocre** chance of succeeding
