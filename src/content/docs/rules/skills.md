---
title: Skills
---

Sometimes success is just a matter of your character's knowledge and ability,  
and there are no **tactical** or **creative** decisions for a player at the
table to make.

You can use a [Fate Roll](/start-here/how-to-play/#fate-roll) to see how well
your character performs their skill.

| Some Example Skills |                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acrobatics          | dodging & tumbling safely                                                                |
| Animal Kinship      | deal with wild animals, connect to your spirit animal,<br />control your lycanthropy     |
| Diplomacy           | persuade crowds of people, create rapport with the high & mighty,<br />command followers |
| Dwarf-craft         | alter & create [Dwarven Artifacts](/rules/magic/dwarven-artifacts)                       |
| Herbalism           | create potent mixtures & gather rare ingredients                                         |
| Hunting             | follow tracks, find food, move stealthily                                                |
| Lockpicking         | pick locks & interact with advanced mechanisms                                           |
| Mercantile          | find a seller of something obscure, haggle for better prices                             |
| Poetry & Music      | humiliate someone in a verbal battle, entertain a crowd, highly praise                   |
| Riding              | control your mount in stressful situations                                               |
| Sailing             | control a ship during battles & dangerous voyages                                        |
| Wandering           | travel well and provide for yourself in the wilderness                                   |
