---
title: Hospitality
---

Everyone must provide food, shelter, and bedding to someone who asks.  
It is a sacred obligation that only villains will violate.

Players seeking hospitality quickly find themselves  
at a social gathering in the home of a friend or stranger.

As a random wanderer you can expect:

- a spot on the floor by the animals
- stale bread & watery mead

However, if you bring gifts/money, have good relationships,  
or a famous reputation:

- a proper bed and a warm fire
- decadent food
- the company of useful and friendly people
